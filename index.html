Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the difference between a UI and UX Designer?

    UX is about functionality and user satisfaction, UI is about visual presentation and aesthetics. They work together: good UX needs good UI, and good UI needs good UX

  • How to become a UI designer?

    Here’s a short guide on how to become a UI designer:
    1 Learn design basics — study color theory, typography, layout, and visual hierarchy.
    2 Master UI tools — get skilled in Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or Photoshop.
    3 Study UI principles — understand consistency, accessibility, responsive design, and design systems.
    4 Build a portfolio — create real or fictional projects (apps, websites) to showcase your skills.
    5 Get feedback — share work on Dribbble, Behance, or with mentors.
    6 Practice & learn continuously — follow trends, take courses (Coursera, Udemy), do design challenges.
    7 Gain experience — do freelance projects, internships, or contribute to open‑source.
    8 Apply for jobs — look for junior UI designer roles or assistant positions.

  • What is the best UI design tool?

    Figma is the most versatile and widely adopted option, but the best choice ultimately depends on your workflow, team structure, and platform requirements.

  • What is the best place to learn Figma?

    One of the best places to learn Figma is Skillbox, which offers a comprehensive course called «Figma form 0 to PRO». This two-month online program is designed for beginners and focuses on practical skills, covering everything from basic tools to advanced features like Auto Layout, Constraints, and animation.

  • Should designers code?

    Designers don’t have to code, but knowing the basics is highly beneficial.
